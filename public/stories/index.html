Their life story, in their own voice

Firsthand Stories records residents' life stories, then gives each family a private website to keep them: chapters, a transcript, and family photos.

How it works

  1. 1

    A conversation

    About an hour, at home or in a quiet room. Skip any question, anytime.

  2. 2

    Edited, never rewritten

    Pauses and filler words trimmed, never a word changed. The resident approves it before anyone else hears it.

  3. 3

    A private site for the family

    Chapter by chapter, with photos, on any phone. Downloads include audio, a PDF transcript with photos, and everything in one file.

What the family receives

Privacy

  • The resident agrees first and can stop anytime.
  • Only the family the resident names receives the story.
  • Nothing is shared publicly without separate written permission.

See the privacy policy for details.
