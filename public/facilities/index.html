Every resident has a story.
Keep it in their voice.

Firsthand Stories gives senior living communities a simple way to record residents’ life stories, set their photos beside them, and share them privately with family.

Why communities do it.

  • Residents feel known. An hour spent telling your life to someone who listens is one of the best hours of a week.
  • Families stay close. Children and grandchildren hear the stories, in the resident’s own voice, on any phone.
  • Staff know who they care for. A story page tells a new caregiver more than a chart can.
  • A program people talk about. Life-story events bring families in and set your community apart.

Two ways to run it.

  • Your team records

    We set your activities or life-enrichment staff up with the Firsthand app on an iPad and a pair of small microphones, train them in an afternoon, and support them as they go. Your community owns the program and the recordings.

  • We record for you

    We come to your community on a schedule, sit down with residents one by one, scan their photos and deliver each story ready to share. Nothing for your staff to learn.

What each resident gets.

  • Their life story, recorded in their own voice, with a chapter for every question.
  • Their photos scanned and set right beside the moments they belong to.
  • A private page that plays on any phone, shared only with the people they choose.
  • A full transcript, and a printed book if they’d like one.
  • Questions shared ahead of time, so family can add their own.

What families receive.

  • On any phone

    Chapters play beside the photos they belong to. Nothing to install.

  • Audio

    Every chapter, in the resident’s own voice.

  • Video slideshow

    Their voice over their photos, for family gatherings.

  • Printable booklet

    Every word in print, beside every photo.

Private by design.

Recordings stay on your community’s devices until a resident or their family chooses to share them. We work with your team on consent and on who can see each story.
