Why communities do it

Stories that bring people closer.

Residents feel known.

An hour spent telling your life to someone who really listens is one of the best hours of a week. Residents choose the stories and the photos; they hear themselves taken seriously.

Families stay close.

Children and grandchildren hear the stories in the resident’s own voice, chapter by chapter, beside the photos, on any phone. No app to install, nothing to sign up for.

Staff know who they care for.

A story page tells a new caregiver more than a chart can: where someone grew up, what they did for a living, who they loved. Better conversations start on day one.

Your name on every story.

Each story page carries your community’s logo and links in its top bar. Families share them with friends, and every share is a warm introduction to you.

Two ways to run it

Your team, or ours.

  • Your team records

    We set your activities or life-enrichment staff up with the Firsthand app and a pair of small microphones, train them in an afternoon, and support them as they go. Your community owns the program and the recordings.

  • We record for you

    We come to your community on a schedule, sit down with residents one by one, scan their photos and deliver each story ready to share. Nothing for your staff to learn.

What residents and families receive

A life story to keep.

  • A private story page

    Their life story in their own voice, a chapter for every question, their photos scanned and set beside the moments they belong to. Shared only with the people they choose.

  • Audio

    Every chapter, in the resident’s own voice. Plays anywhere.

  • Video slideshow

    Their voice over their photos, for family gatherings.

  • Transcript and booklet

    Every word in print beside every photo, and a hardcover book if they’d like one.

Private by design

Their story, their choice.

Recordings stay on your community’s devices and your own iCloud until a resident or their family chooses to share them. We work with your team on consent and on who can see each story.
