The trouble with shoeboxes.

Every family has one. Most never get opened.

  • Photos nobody sees.

    Albums in the closet. Slides in a carousel. Negatives in an envelope nobody can look through.

  • Stories nobody wrote down.

    Who’s that next to Grandpa? Where was that house? You know. Nobody else does.

  • Never enough time.

    Sorting, scanning, interviewing, editing. It’s on the someday list. Someday is busy.

  • Recordings nobody hears.

    A two-hour phone video with no way to find the good part isn’t much of a keepsake.

Questions.

Am I too young for this?

No. Most people tell their story best in their 50s, 60s and 70s, while names, dates and details come easily. You can always add chapters later.

Can it be ready in time for the holidays?

Yes, if we record by mid-December. If not, give a gift certificate and we’ll record in the new year at the holiday price.

How long should the interview be?

An hour covers the highlights. Three hours covers a life, from childhood to today. Longer conversations work best in sessions of an hour or so.

Who can hear it?

Only the people you choose. Your story page is private, and the recording belongs to your family.

Can my family join in?

Yes. They can send questions ahead of time, sit in, or add photos of their own.

What if I’m not sure what to say?

You’ll see the questions ahead of time and can skip any of them. It’s a conversation, not a performance.
