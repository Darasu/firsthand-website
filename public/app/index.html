What it does.

The hard parts, handled.

  • Ask, and it’s a chapter.

    Tap each question as you ask it. Later, skip straight to the farm, the first job or the move across the world.

  • A template for every talk.

    Personal History, A Veteran’s Story, An Immigration Story, Job Interview, Podcast Guest and more. Or write your own.

  • Two mics. Or just the one.

    You and your guest on separate tracks with a pair of wireless lapel mics. Or simply the iPhone’s own microphone.

  • Finished, not just recorded.

    A transcript, gentle clean-up of pauses and ums, and exports as chaptered audio, a video slideshow or a PDF.

  • Take five. Or five days.

    Older guests need rest. Pick up another day, with a recap of where you left off.

  • Private by design.

    Everything stays on your device. No account, no ads, no tracking. Transcription happens right on your iPhone.
