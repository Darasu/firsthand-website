Privacy Policy

Firsthand · Effective October 11, 2026 · Updated November 4, 2026

This policy covers the Firsthand app. Story sites made for Firsthand Stories clients are covered in Story sites, below.

In short (the app): Firsthand keeps your interviews on your devices and, if you use iCloud Sync, in your own iCloud. We don't collect, see or sell any of your information unless you choose Publish to Web or Create Prep Page, there's no account to create to use it, and the app has no ads, analytics or tracking.

What stays on your devices

Everything you create in Firsthand is stored only on your iPhone, iPad or Mac, and in your own iCloud if iCloud Sync is on:

  • Your recordings, and any recording of a guest saying their name or agreeing to be recorded
  • Transcripts, chapters and notes
  • Names, photos, places and other interview details you enter
  • Your question templates

For the Firsthand app, we don't run servers that receive this information, and Firsthand never uploads your interviews anywhere but your own iCloud, except one you choose to publish (see Publish to Web, below) and the questions of one you choose to send a Prep Page for (see Prep Page, below). Transcription, speaker detection and the drafting of job interview questions from a resume all happen on your device.

Permissions the app asks for

  • Microphone: to record your interviews, check your microphones in Recording Setup or when you plug one in, and record a guest saying their name or agreeing to be recorded. Audio is saved only on your device.
  • Camera: only if you choose to take a photo for an interview. Photos are saved only on your device.
  • Location: only if you ask to use where you are when choosing an interview's place. It's used to show the map and find the place's name; Firsthand doesn't track your location.

You can change any of these at any time in your device's Settings. Firsthand works without camera and location access; it needs the microphone to record.

Apple services the app uses

A few features rely on Apple's own services, which are covered by Apple's Privacy Policy:

  • Maps: when you search for a place or use your current location, the search or location is sent to Apple to find matching places. Firsthand only keeps the place you choose, on your device.
  • Speech recognition: the first time you transcribe, iOS may download Apple's on-device speech model. Your audio isn't sent anywhere; transcription runs on your device.
  • Calendar: Schedule Next Session opens Calendar's own event editor, so the event is saved by you, in Calendar. Firsthand can't read your calendar; it keeps only the date you chose, on your device.
  • Apple Intelligence: Tailor to a Resume uses Apple's on-device language model. The resume is read on your device and isn't stored afterwards; only the questions you keep are saved.

Looking up a podcast guest

Tailor to the Guest can draft questions about a guest from public information. If you search for a guest by name, the name is sent to Wikipedia to find their article, and the article (and a photo, if it has one) is downloaded. If you give a link instead, only that web page is opened. Nothing about your interviews is sent, and the questions are drafted on your device. These requests go straight from your device to Wikipedia or the site you chose, never to us.

When you share

Prep Page

Create Prep Page is for Firsthand Stories staff, with the same sign-in as Publish to Web. Only when you choose Create Prep Page for an interview that hasn't begun does the app send its questions (with their sections and story titles), the interview's title, and the guest's and interviewer's names to Firsthand Stories, where they become a page at a private link for the guest and their family. Anyone with the link can open the page, mark questions to skip, write notes under a story, add questions and add photos (with captions) to a story, each with a name if they give one; that is kept with the page and fetched by the app when you open the upcoming interview. Once the interview begins, skips, notes and questions can't be changed, but photos can still be added; the app brings each photo onto its story in the interview. Deleting the upcoming interview in the app deletes the page and everything written on it. Nothing else about the interview is sent: no recording, photos, place or notes.

Publish to Web

Publish to Web is for Firsthand Stories staff. Only when you choose Publish for an interview does the app upload that interview's audio (one file per question), its photos, its transcript, a PDF of it, the guest's name and the interview's notes, date and place to Firsthand Stories, where it becomes a private story site (see Story sites). To sign in, your email address is sent to us and we email you a one-time code; the app keeps the sign-in in your device's keychain until you sign out in Settings › Web Publishing. If you enter the guest's email address, it's sent too, so they can sign in as the story's owner and choose who else can see it. Nothing else about your interviews is sent, and nothing is sent until you choose Publish.

If you export or share an interview (audio, video, a PDF or text version, or an interview file to back up or move to another device), it goes wherever you choose to send it, such as Messages, Mail, Files or another app. What happens to it there is governed by that service.

iCloud Sync

With iCloud Sync on (Settings › iCloud Sync, on by default), your interviews, recordings and templates are kept in step on your devices signed in to the same Apple Account, through Apple's iCloud. They're stored in your private iCloud space, which only you can reach; we can't see or read them. Turn iCloud Sync off and Firsthand keeps everything on that device only.

Backups

Like other apps, Firsthand's data can be included in your device's backups, such as iCloud Backup if you've turned it on. Those backups are managed by Apple under your Apple Account. Firsthand has no access to them.

Recording other people

Firsthand records the people you interview. Please make sure everyone being recorded knows it and agrees, and follow the laws where you are about recording conversations. The app reminds you of this before you record.

Deleting your information

Delete an interview in the app and it moves to Recently Deleted, where you can recover it for 30 days; after that, or when you delete it there, its recording, transcript and details are removed from your device. Deleting the app removes everything Firsthand stored.

Children

Firsthand isn't directed at children under 13 and doesn't knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update it here and change the date at the top. If a change ever affected how your information is handled, we'd say so in the app first.

Story sites (Firsthand Stories service)

This section covers Firsthand Stories, a separate service in which we record and edit a person's life story in person and give their family a private website to keep it. The rest of this policy covers the Firsthand app.

  • Consent: the person telling their story agrees before anything is recorded, and can stop at any time.
  • Who receives it: only the family members the storyteller names receive the story.
  • Nothing public: nothing is shared publicly without separate written permission.
  • The community: the senior living community sees a story only if the storyteller gives separate written permission.

What we keep

Each story site holds the edited audio, the transcript (including the PDF version), the family photos chosen for it, and any corrections family members make to the transcript's wording on the site. Corrections are saved with the story and included in the transcript and downloads.

We keep the original recordings and files on our own iPhone, iPad and computer, in our own iCloud, and on a backup drive we keep. We keep these copies until we're asked to delete the story (see below).

Where story sites are hosted

Story sites are hosted by Cloudflare (Workers, D1 and R2 storage, and Email Sending for sign-in codes), which handles the files under Cloudflare's Privacy Policy.

Signing in

Family members sign in with their email address and a 6-digit code we email them. The code expires after 10 minutes, and a sign-in lasts 30 days. A cookie in the browser keeps them signed in; we keep only a scrambled (hashed) copy of codes and sign-ins, never the codes themselves.

The family's email addresses are kept in the story's list of people who can sign in, which the story's owner manages on the site. We use them to let family members sign in and to contact the family about their story, and we remove them when the story site is deleted.

Analytics, cookies and other companies

Cloudflare Web Analytics counts visits to story sites, such as how often pages are viewed; Cloudflare says it doesn't use cookies for this. Apart from Cloudflare Access's sign-in cookie, story sites set no cookies. The page remembers two display settings in your browser: whether the transcript is shown beneath the photo, and whether a community's name is shown at the top. Story sites show no ads, and apart from Cloudflare we don't share story sites or their visitors' information with other companies.

A story site made for a community's residents may show the community's name and links to its website at the top. Those links lead to the community's own site, which has its own privacy policy.

How long a story site stays online

A story site stays online while its hosting is paid for. When the story is delivered, the family also receives their own copy of everything: the audio, a PDF transcript with photos, and all of it in one file. The site is a convenient way to share and listen, not the only copy.

If hosting is no longer paid for, we email the family 30 days before the site comes down. Then we delete the story site and every copy we keep, including backups, and remove the family's email addresses.

Deleting a story

The storyteller can ask us to delete their story by emailing privacy@firsthandstories.app. If the storyteller has died or can no longer decide, the family members they named can ask instead. Within 48 hours we take the story site down, delete its recordings, transcript and photos, and remove the family's email addresses from the sign-in list. Within 30 days we delete every copy we keep, including backups. Files the family has already downloaded are theirs to keep. Cloudflare's sign-in records are kept by Cloudflare under its own retention rules.
