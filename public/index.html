Why now, and not someday?

Most people wait until it's harder. The best time is while you're busy living.

  • You're the one who knows

    Who's in that photo, where that house was, why you took that job. Only you can tell it, and it's best told while it's fresh.

  • Photos nobody sees

    Albums in the closet, slides in a carousel, negatives in an envelope. We scan them and you tell us the story behind each one.

  • You talk, we do the rest

    No writing, no technology to learn. We plan, record, scan, edit and put it all together. You just tell the stories.

How it works

In person, at their pace.

  1. 1

    A free planning call

    Fifteen minutes to choose the questions together. You see them ahead of time, and your family can add their own.

  2. 2

    A relaxed conversation

    At your home or in your community, with professional microphones. Skip any question; several short sessions are fine.

  3. 3

    Edited, never rewritten

    Long pauses and "um"s trimmed, never a word changed. You approve it before anyone else hears it.

  4. 4

    Yours to share

    A private page for the people you choose, and a printed book if you'd like one.

For retirement communities

A life-story program your residents will talk about.

  • On site. We record in a quiet room at your community, on a schedule that suits residents and staff.
  • For active residents. Most people tell their story best in their 50s, 60s and 70s, while names, dates and details come easily. Residents of any age are welcome.
  • Something to share. Each resident gets a private story page for their family. With permission, stories can be played at a gathering or shared in your newsletter.
  • No work for staff. We handle sign-ups, scheduling, recording and every finished page.
